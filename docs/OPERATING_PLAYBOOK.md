# Operating playbook

## Daily
- Check active replies and pending customer deliverables first.
- Identify 5–10 verifiable UAE marketplace sellers with product and listing evidence.
- Score fit, urgency, reachable public business contact, and duplication risk.
- Prepare the top three tailored introductions; use approved sending pathways only.
- Record statuses: discovered, qualified, drafted, approved, sent-confirmed, replied, won, lost.
- Escalate interested replies, payment requests and any uncertain claims to the owner.

## Weekly
Review prospect-to-reply, reply-to-call, call-to-sale, delivery completion and collected revenue. Prioritize actions with demonstrable impact.

## Delivery standard
An AED 299 audit includes evidence-backed listing observations, prioritized improvements, and PPC recommendations. Never claim access to private advertising data unless supplied by the customer.
