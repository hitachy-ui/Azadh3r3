# Money Machine 2.0

A human-supervised AI business operating system for finding customers, converting legitimate demand, delivering useful services, and measuring outcomes.

## Initial commercial mission
Sell a UAE marketplace listing and PPC audit for AED 299. Prioritize the first verified paying customer over vanity activity.

## Operating cycle
1. Discover public business prospects with relevant marketplace listings.
2. Qualify and deduplicate prospects against the lead ledger.
3. Produce individualized, truthful outreach drafts with evidence.
4. Send only through authorized connected channels and log confirmed outcomes.
5. Monitor replies and prepare proposals.
6. Deliver an evidence-backed audit; collect feedback.
7. Review funnel metrics weekly and refine.

## Safeguards
- Never claim a message was sent or a payment received without evidence.
- No spam, scraped private contact details, fabricated testimonials, guaranteed results, or paid actions without approval.
- No secrets or personal customer data in this public repository.
- Human approval gates for sensitive external actions.
- Automations are not guaranteed to run without their connected services and permissions.

## Project structure
- `config/mission.json`: goals, limits and KPI definitions.
- `docs/OPERATING_PLAYBOOK.md`: workflow and acceptance criteria.
- `data/leads.example.csv`: empty illustrative ledger schema.

## Deployment status
Planning scaffold only. No live service, scheduled worker, CRM integration or outreach has been deployed.
